# Unreleased changes

Draft of changes awaiting release. Selected changes are planned for 1.1.3.
Reporting with /wdr is saved for a later release, and nickname sharing is not
available online yet. Release requirements and verification details are kept in
Development notes below.

## New features

- Windows installer users can download updates in Fury and choose when to restart.
- AutoDenick can suggest possible real names from final kills or broken beds before checking both to confirm a match.
- Automatically save a teammate's nickname when only one party member can match, with Undo.
- Choose whether to share your nickname while nicked with Fury players in your party, and automatically save nicknames they share.
- See your current and previously saved nicknames with /denick self.
- Report up to eight players with /wdr without opening the report menu, then see which reports succeeded.

## Improvements

- Urchin checks tags in small groups while available player stats keep loading.
- Tab Stats shows available stats early, and Scan shows everyone together once checks finish.
- Denick searches check more players and compare final kills and broken beds more carefully before confirming a match.
- Denick messages show stats and tags together in chat, with fewer repeated notices.
- AutoDenick announces when it starts checking finals, beds, or both, once per player for each check.
- Auto Dodge ignores Replays, Account and Blacklisted tags, and only dodges Caution when cheats are listed.
- Choose whether Auto Dodge leaves instantly or just before the game starts.
- Copy your API keys and Minecraft addresses with a Copy button.
- Reset to defaults now restores all three preset Minecraft connections.
- Player stats recover from more temporary loading problems and keep already loaded stats visible.

## Bug fixes

- Tab Stats no longer gets stuck or loses loaded stats when Hypixel refreshes the player list.
- Quick Duels and rematches now count toward your session.
- Finished games, kills and broken beds stay saved when stats arrive late or you disconnect, end or reset a session.
- Refreshing stats no longer starts a new session during a game.
- Session stats no longer use another account's results.
- Player names above heads in Duels no longer turn white during a game.
- Auto Dodge no longer cancels a planned leave during the final Bed Wars countdown warning.
- Automatic skin and stats denicks no longer reveal party members in party chat.
